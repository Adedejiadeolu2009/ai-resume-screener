About Aptura AI

Resume screening built for faster, clearer hiring decisions.

Aptura AI helps recruiters, founders, HR teams, and growing businesses move from resume overload to a ranked shortlist. It reads resumes against a role description, highlights fit signals, flags gaps, and keeps previous screening sessions available in your dashboard.

What the product focuses on

The goal is not to replace human hiring judgement. The goal is to reduce first-pass review time and make every candidate comparison more consistent.

Why we built it

Hiring teams often receive more resumes than they can review properly. Strong candidates can be missed because formatting is different, keywords are buried, or reviewers are under time pressure. Aptura AI creates a structured first pass so teams can compare candidates with clearer evidence before deciding who to interview.

What Aptura AI does

Ranks candidates

Upload resumes with a job description and receive a ranked list based on experience, technical fit, education, communication signals, leadership, and role alignment.

Explains the score

Each result includes strengths, gaps, interview questions, red flags, key skills, and a short executive summary so your team can review quickly.

Keeps hiring history

Your dashboard tracks previous screenings, total candidates processed, top candidates, recommendations, and analytics across your hiring activity.

Packages

Start free, then upgrade by bank transfer when your hiring volume increases. After admin confirms payment, the selected package is activated and a confirmation email is sent to the email address on the user's account.
