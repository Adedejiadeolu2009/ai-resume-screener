Trust Layer

Aptura can recommend. You decide.

Career and hiring intelligence only works when people can see the evidence, understand what a recommendation means, and keep the final decision with a person.

Open trust settingsReview agent architecture
audit trail
  • Human approval before profile changes or sensitive actions.
  • Evidence-backed recommendations with clear gaps and uncertainty.
  • No silent profile changes from AI tools or browser agents.
  • Resume analysis is tied to the authenticated account that submitted it.
  • Only upload material you are authorized to share; recruiter recommendations require review before action.

Verification is specific.

Aptura does not use one generic badge for everything. Candidate, employer, recruiter, job, document, assessment, institution, and employer evidence need separate states.

Candidate controls

Your saved resume and career analysis are available only after sign-in. Aptura proposes changes; it does not overwrite your resume automatically.

Recruiter controls

Candidate rankings are decision support, not automatic rejection or hiring. Review the source evidence and interview candidates before acting.

AI controls

Scores summarize information from the submitted resume and role description. They are not verification of skills, identity, eligibility, or future performance.
