# Aptura Product Sequence

Aptura should focus on becoming a two-sided talent intelligence platform before becoming a general job board.

## Positioning

Aptura helps candidates present credible evidence and helps employers make faster, more explainable hiring decisions.

> Candidates build evidence. Employers evaluate evidence. Aptura coordinates the match.

## Sequence 1: Evidence Layer

**Goal:** Make the Career Passport the candidate's durable identity on Aptura.

Build:

- Candidate-approved portfolio links and project evidence.
- Resume and profile evidence in one structured record.
- Clear evidence states: self-reported, document supplied, assessment verified, institution verified, and employer verified.
- Consent, visibility, revoke, and delete controls for every connected source.
- Candidate-owned corrections and human approval before AI-generated changes are saved.

Success signals:

- Candidates can complete a useful profile without applying to a job.
- Employers can understand why a skill appears on a profile.
- No portfolio source is imported or changed without explicit candidate approval.

## Sequence 2: Recruiter Automation

**Goal:** Automate repetitive application handling while preserving human hiring decisions.

Build:

- Role intake and structured job requirements.
- Application collection from uploads, forms, and approved integrations.
- Resume and portfolio evidence extraction.
- Explainable matching, ranking, gap summaries, and shortlist workflows.
- Recruiter-configured web assessments and AI interview sessions.
- Audit trails, bias checks, permissions, and human review gates.

Success signals:

- A recruiter can move from role creation to a reviewable shortlist in one workflow.
- Every recommendation links back to evidence or an explicit missing signal.
- AI never silently rejects a candidate or makes the final hiring decision.

## Sequence 3: Lightweight Verified Job Discovery

**Goal:** Help candidates discover relevant opportunities without becoming a broad job marketplace yet.

Build:

- Curated and verified employer roles only.
- Candidate-controlled matching against Career Passport evidence.
- Match explanations and missing-signal guidance before applying.
- Simple save, share, and application handoff flows.
- Employer quality and role freshness checks.

Success signals:

- Job discovery strengthens matching and applications instead of becoming a separate product.
- Candidates see fewer, more relevant roles with useful preparation guidance.
- Employers receive candidates with clearer evidence and expectations.

## Sequence 4: Marketplace Expansion

**Gate:** Start only after the first three sequences show repeat usage and enough verified employer activity.

Potential additions:

- Larger employer job marketplace.
- Candidate application tracking and communication workflows.
- Employer subscriptions based on screened applications, assessments, and integrations.
- Partner and API distribution.
- Talent pools and permissioned referrals.

Do not build this sequence first. A job marketplace needs liquidity, moderation, trust, notifications, and support operations that can distract from Aptura's strongest initial advantage: evidence-backed matching and screening.

## Product Guardrails

- Aptura recommends; people decide.
- Portfolio collection is consent-based, not silent scraping.
- AI-generated resume, profile, interview, and hiring outputs require appropriate human review.
- Verification status is always visible and never inferred as fact without evidence.
- Candidate data access is permissioned, auditable, and revocable.
