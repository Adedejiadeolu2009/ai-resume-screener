Why we built it

Hiring teams often receive more resumes than they can review properly, while candidates often struggle to understand why a good resume is not matching a target role. Aptura AI focuses on both sides of that moment: clearer candidate comparison for recruiters and clearer next steps for applicants.

What Aptura AI does

Ranks candidates for recruiters

Upload resumes with a job description and receive a ranked list based on experience, technical fit, education, communication signals, leadership, and role alignment.

Explains fit and gaps

Each result includes strengths, gaps, interview questions, red flags, key skills, and a short executive summary so your team can review quickly.

Guides job candidates

The career agent analyzes resumes, compares target roles, identifies skill gaps, and drafts application material from the candidate's profile.

Packages

Start free, then upgrade by bank transfer when your hiring volume increases. After admin confirms payment, the selected package is activated and a confirmation email is sent to the email address on the user's account.
